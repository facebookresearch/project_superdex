# Unitree G1 (29 DoF, rev 1.0)

`generate.py` writes everything here from unitree_rl_mjlab @1425b15 (Apache-2.0; tarball sha256
`186d70765121c2aa356798cc9c51c0e29d0027891b72fce62856b3111c79ad4d`): `src/assets/robots/unitree_g1/xmls/g1.xml`
and `g1_constants.py`. It reads the MJCF as XML text and runs nothing of the upstream repo.

```sh
python3 generate.py --xml <unpacked tarball>/unitree_rl_mjlab/src/assets/robots/unitree_g1/xmls/g1.xml
```

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `unitree_g1.superdex_bot` | 30 links of g1.xml (33.34 kg) with a free pelvis, 29 revolute joints, 4 sites (`imu_in_pelvis`, `imu_in_torso`, `left_foot`, `right_foot`) and 14 foot collider spheres per foot |
| `unitree_g1_box_feet.superdex_bot` | The same with one box per foot in place of its 14 spheres |
| `tasks/g1_velocity_flat/g1_velocity.superdex_scene`'s `LAB_JOINT_ACTUATORS` init args | Written by the generator: g1_constants.py's six BuiltinPositionActuatorCfg as mjlab-style `position` actuators (joint patterns, stiffness, damping, effort limit, armature) |
| `shapes/` | Tet meshes of the 1 cm collider sphere (icosphere level 2 fanned to its center) and of the foot box |

## Where every constant comes from

| Constant | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Masses, COMs, inertias | per body | g1.xml `<inertial>` (`diaginertia` rotated by its `quat` into body axes) |
| Joint frames, axes, limits | per joint | g1.xml `<body pos quat>`, `<joint axis range>`; every joint sits at its body's origin |
| Armature (actuators) | 5020 0.003610, 7520-14 0.010178, 7520-22 0.025102, 4010 0.004250; waist roll/pitch and ankles 2 x 5020 | g1_constants.py: rotor inertias and gears through `reflected_inertia_from_two_stage_planetary` |
| Kp, Kd | armature (2π 10 Hz)², 2 · 2.0 · armature · 2π 10 Hz | g1_constants.py `NATURAL_FREQ`, `DAMPING_RATIO` |
| Effort limits | 25 / 88 / 139 / 5 N m; 50 for the 2 x 5020 joints | g1_constants.py `ElectricActuator` |
| Default pose | hip_pitch -0.1, knee 0.3, ankle_pitch -0.2, shoulder_pitch 0.35, elbow 0.87, shoulder_roll ±0.18 | g1_constants.py `HOME_KEYFRAME` (pelvis at z 0.8: the scene's `parentFromBot`) |
| Joint limit stiffness | 1000 N m/rad | Ours: MuJoCo limits are soft constraints with no stiffness to copy; ~10x the stiffest PD |
| Joint friction, damping | 0 | g1.xml and mjlab set none; the position actuator's damping is implicit, as MuJoCo's position actuator's `kv` |

The bot's joints carry no armature or effort limit, as in g1.xml: the actuators set them.

## Deviations from g1.xml

- **Colliders.** Mochi has no capsule collider and a link has one shape. Each foot capsule (radius 1 cm) becomes
  a sphere at each end, each on a massless child link of `*_ankle_roll_link` under a Hard joint: on flat ground,
  MuJoCo's capsule-plane contact is those two points. `--body-colliders` turns each of the 19 body capsules and
  spheres into spheres no more than one radius apart (off by default: the default labs collide the feet only).
- **Layers.** Foot spheres are layer `G1Foot`, body spheres `G1Body`; the G1 scenes disable contact within and
  between these layers, so only the ground touches them. This is a deviation in every G1 lab, the parity lab
  included: unitree_rl_mjlab's G1 entity uses `FULL_COLLISION` (`g1_constants.py`, `get_g1_robot_cfg`). Under it,
  every `*_collision` geom touches the ground and the robot's other links (condim 1, so frictionless, except
  foot-to-foot; MuJoCo skips parent-child pairs), and the velocity task penalizes self contact
  (`self_collisions`, weight -1, substeps above 10 N). Our labs build the bot without body colliders, so only the
  feet touch the ground, and nothing collides with itself. mjlab's `FEET_ONLY_COLLISION` comes closest to what
  our labs do, but no unitree_rl_mjlab G1 task uses it.
- **Contact parameters.** Mochi penalty contact, not MuJoCo's soft constraints. The G1 ground plane
  (`tasks/g1_velocity_flat/scene/g1_flat.mochi_scene`) sets a 1 mm smoothing half-band in place of Mochi's 5 mm (the
  band comes from the collider, the plane, which only the G1's feet touch): standing, the 1 cm foot spheres sink
  0.8 mm (3.6 mm at the default). Foot friction: the foot colliders' 0.36 against the ground's 1 is 0.6, g1_constants.py's (Mochi takes the geometric mean). Stiffness is Mochi's default for now; flagged for the
  foot-ground pair once labs set contact pairs.
- **Render models.** None yet (training needs none).
