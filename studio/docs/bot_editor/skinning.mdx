# Skinning

A bot can carry a **skin**: one continuous surface stretched over its links,
such as the soft covering of a hand. The skin follows the links as they move,
and can stand in for them in contact, in rendering, or in both. A skinned hand
can touch an object with one smooth, deforming palm surface instead of a set of
separate rigid link shapes, and can render as one seamless mesh.

A skin has two optional models, and each has its own role:

| Model | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Mochi Model** | The skin's collision model (`.mochi.h5`). Other bodies collide with this deforming surface. |
| **Render Model** | A skinned `.glb` whose bones follow the bot's links. It is drawn in place of the link render models it covers. |

## The Bot Skin Window

Open **Window → Bot Skin** in the Bot Editor. For a bot without a skin, press
**Add Skin**; **Remove Skin** takes it off again.

- **Model**: drop the skin's **Mochi Model** and **Render Model** from the Asset
  Browser into their slots. The render model has its own translation,
  rotation, and scale.
- **Collision / Contact**: the skin's **Layer**, its contact parameters, and how
  its surface is sampled for contact, with the same meaning as a link's
  [Collision / Contact](./editing.mdx#link) settings.
- **Skin Link Substitution**: one row per link. Tick **Collision** to turn off
  that link's own contact and let the skin collide in its place. Tick
  **Render** to hide the link's render model wherever the skin is drawn. By
  default every **Collision** box is ticked, so the skin is the bot's only
  collider, and the skin hides the render models of the links it is skinned
  over.

**Show → Skin-Covered Links** in the viewport toggles drawing the links the
skin hides, so you can check what lies underneath. Press `Space` to simulate:
the skin deforms with the links, and force-dragging works on the skinned bot
as on any other.

## Skins on Mod Bots

A [Mod Bot](./modding.mdx) adds a skin with an **AttachSkin** modification
instead. In the Bot Skin window of a Mod Bot, **Add Skin Modification** appends
one and the window edits it; a skin that comes from a referenced bot is shown
as read-only, to be edited in that bot.

## Authoring the Render Model

**Bot → Export Skeletal GLB...** writes the bot as a skeletal `.glb`, a
skeleton that follows the bot's link hierarchy, which you can then skin in a
tool such as Blender.
Choose whether to include the **Render meshes**, the **Collision meshes**, both,
or neither (the joint hierarchy alone), and bind the skeleton to the bot's
**Rest** (zero) pose or **Bake current pose**.
